How I use Generative AI in my daily dev workflow without letting it ruin my codebase:

1. Architecture & state management: Designed manually first.
2. Boilerplate & regex: Handed off to the LLM with strict context.
3. Debugging: Feed the exact stack trace + isolated function, never the whole repo blindly.

Treat AI like a fast pair-programmer, not a replacement for fundamentals.
